# ZOL Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 7, 2026

ZOL provides an AI voice and messaging assistant for mechanic shops and event booth visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how SMS communications work.

## Information We Collect

When you call or interact with ZOL, we may collect:

- Your name
- Your phone number
- SMS consent status
- Vehicle details you provide, such as year, make, model, mileage, symptoms, and service needs
- Appointment preferences and booked appointment details
- Call or message content needed to answer questions, send requested links, prepare estimates, or schedule appointments

## How We Use Information

We use this information to:

- Answer questions about ZOL
- Send requested booking links
- Send repair estimates or appointment confirmations by SMS
- Schedule appointments
- Notify the ZOL team about confirmed appointments or follow-up needs
- Improve our service and customer experience

## SMS Communications

ZOL sends SMS messages only when a caller requests information or verbally agrees to receive a text. The AI voice agent confirms the phone number and asks permission before sending SMS messages.

Messages may include founder booking links, repair estimate summaries, appointment confirmations, reminders, or follow-up information requested during a call.

Message frequency varies based on your requests and appointment activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply **STOP** to opt out. Reply **HELP** for help.

## Sharing Information

ZOL does not sell or rent personal information. We do not share SMS opt-in consent or phone numbers with third parties for their marketing purposes.

We may share information only with service providers needed to operate ZOL, such as voice, SMS, calendar, hosting, and shop workflow systems. These providers are used only to deliver the requested service.

## Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

## Data Retention

We retain information only as long as needed to provide service, maintain records, improve operations, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

## Your Choices

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying **STOP**. For help, reply **HELP** or contact ZOL through the phone number used to communicate with you.
